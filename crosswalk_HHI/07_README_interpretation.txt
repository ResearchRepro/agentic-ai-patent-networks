1. This is a NEW exploratory fractional cross-walk, not the previous Tableau cross-walk or its earlier p-values.
2. Patent-to-technology: source lexical profile fractions, not exclusive labels.
3. Patent-to-organization: equal 1/k credit for each distinct applicant, then aggregated to all coapplication Louvain communities; eight largest communities by node count are displayed, with other connected and no-coapplication labels retained.
4. One eligible family contributes exactly one patent-equivalent; same-patent applicants are NOT assumed to collaborate because of single-applicant filings. Coapplication edges remain separate evidence.
5. Both permutation nulls retain whole technology vectors, preserve all patent-group vectors, and use a Monte Carlo (+1)/(B+1) p-value; secondary null stratifies by single- versus multi-applicant families.
6. Individual cell tests are two-sided, adjusted using Benjamini-Hochberg across 70 combinations for each null. Global test uses observed cross-walk versus permutation distribution; no asymptotic chi-square p-value.
7. Applicant HHIs use patent credits over the available cohort, expressed 0–10000, original released labels and sensitivity using only 50 reviewed positive name pairs. Fractional credit sums to one per patent. Full counting is a sensitivity method and has a different denominator.
8. Patent-level HHI is NOT industrial market concentration and is NOT evidence of supply-chain vulnerability.
9. This analysis cannot validate the earlier 8.5% open-source overlap because open-source project sampling/matching files are absent. Do not cite the 8.5% as verified from this bundle.
10. Permutations do not fully account for repeated applicant portfolios, patent family relationships across group labels or applicant degree heterogeneity; interpret p-values conditionally.
11. Do not copy numerical results into a revised manuscript before author validation of the normalization and analysis scope.
